# Things to ask

Once the Fokals MCP is connected, ask in plain words. Your assistant picks the tool, and every answer says what it cost.

## Research one account
- "Tell me everything about acme.com before my call tomorrow."
- "What technologies does acme.com run, and which did it add lately?"
- "Is acme.com hiring? Which teams, and where?"

## Enrich your list
- "Enrich these companies: acme.com, globex.com, initech.com."
- "Which of these companies run HubSpot, and which are hiring sales roles?"

## Find companies by signal
- "How many B2B software companies in the US run Salesforce and are hiring now? Don't fetch them yet."
- "Find online shops on Shopify that added Klaviyo this month."
- "Companies showing signs of buying marketing automation, in Germany."
- "Companies that announced funding in the last two weeks."

## Watch and compare
- "Find companies like acme.com, and say what they have in common."
- "Watch acme.com and globex.com and email me when something changes."
- "What changed at these five companies this month?"

## Hiring
- "Open data engineer roles asking for Snowflake, remote, in the UK."
- "Which companies started hiring account executives this month?"
